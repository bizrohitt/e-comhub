# COPY BIBLE — The House Of Sahas

**Status:** skeleton, Phase 0. Lines below marked *DRAFT* are the founder's direction examples from Section 11 and are not yet approved copy.
Filled in task `P0-06`.

**Working rule:** whenever copy is written, give **two variants**, say which is preferred and why (Master Prompt Section 11).

---

## 1. Voice

- Calm, confident, refined, slightly literary.
- Short sentences.
- Think heritage tailor, not fast fashion.
- The visitor should feel: "I have walked into an established house with history and standards."
- Promise line: **"Dress like you have always belonged in the room."**

## 2. Banned

**Punctuation and symbols:** no exclamation marks. No emojis.

**Hype words (never use):** amazing · killer · must-have · awesome · incredible · stunning · best-ever · game-changing · unbeatable · lowest price · blowout · insane · crazy deal · don't miss out · hurry · last chance (unless literally true) · limited time (unless literally true).

**Tactics banned outright:** fake countdowns, invented scarcity, manufactured urgency, unverified testimonials, unverified press claims, discount shouting. Everything on the site must be true (Master Prompt Section 10). Fake scarcity destroys trust and may violate consumer protection law.

**Also avoid:** all-caps sentences, multiple CTAs competing in one section, copy that explains a joke, adjectives stacked three deep.

## 3. Preferred vocabulary

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| Add to cart | Add to Wardrobe |
| Cart | Your Selection |
| Checkout | Complete Your Order |
| Buy now | Reserve / Acquire |
| Customers | Clients, Members of the House |
| Sale / Discount | (avoid; use value-added offers instead) |
| Shop | The Collections / The House |
| Newsletter | The House Circle |

## 4. Headline formulas (to be expanded in P0-06)

1. **Belonging:** place the reader in the room they want to be in.
2. **Craft statement:** a plain declarative about how the thing is made.
3. **Inheritance:** the piece outlives the season.
4. **Quiet correction:** a short line that implies the loud alternative is beneath us.

## 6. Product description structure (fixed order, no filler)

1. One-line essence.
2. Fabric and origin (mill name where true).
3. Fit and cut.
4. Occasion.
5. Care.
